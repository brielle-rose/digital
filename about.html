About Behind the Smiles

More than just a smile. A place to talk about the things we sometimes keep hidden — without judgement.

Why this matters

You don't have to pretend you're okay.

Behind the Smiles is a supportive website for young people who may feel pressure to keep everyone happy, avoid conflict or be the “perfect” friend, student or family member.

People-pleasing can sometimes feel like kindness, but it can also leave you exhausted, worried about disappointing others or unsure what you really want. This website offers information, reflection and small steps that may help.

♡ No judgement ◇ Honest conversations ✿ Personal growth

This website is for learning and support. It is not a replacement for advice from a trusted adult or mental health professional.
