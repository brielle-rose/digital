What is People Pleasing?

People pleasing is when someone puts other people's needs, feelings or opinions before their own.

It can feel easier to say yes, avoid conflict or make others happy, even when you don't really want to.

Why does it happen?

People may become people pleasers because they are afraid of rejection, disappointing others or being judged.
