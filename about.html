Get to know us

Understanding the person behind the smile.

SafeSpace is a place to learn about people-pleasing and the pressure many young people feel to make everyone else happy.

Our purpose

Why SafeSpace exists

Many young people feel pressure to fit in, meet expectations, avoid disappointing others, and appear as though everything is okay.

Sometimes, this can lead us to hide our feelings or ignore what we need. SafeSpace aims to help young people recognise these patterns and understand that their wellbeing matters, too.

This website is educational and supportive. It is not about blaming people for caring, but about finding a healthier balance between caring for others and caring for yourself.

REMEMBER

You are allowed to take up space.

You don't have to earn your worth.

Let's understand it

What is people-pleasing?

People-pleasing can involve regularly putting other people's wishes ahead of your own because you want their approval or fear their disappointment.

♡

Caring for others

Being kind, helpful, and considerate is a good thing. Healthy relationships allow everyone to share their needs and feelings.

↔

Losing yourself in others

It may become a problem when you regularly say yes out of fear, feel responsible for everyone's emotions, or neglect your own needs.
