Behind the Smiles

Understanding what it means to always put other people first.

What is people-pleasing?

People-pleasing is when someone regularly puts other people's needs, feelings or expectations before their own.

It can look like saying yes when you want to say no, avoiding conflict, worrying about disappointing people or changing yourself so others will accept you.

Why does it matter?

Caring about other people is a good thing. However, constantly ignoring your own needs can become exhausting.

Learning about people-pleasing can help young people recognise their own feelings and understand that their needs matter too.
