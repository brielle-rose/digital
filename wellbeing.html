Your mind matters

Wellbeing

Your mind. Your peace. Your priority. Find gentle ideas to support your mental, emotional and everyday health.

Gentle tools

Care for your mind, body and feelings.

You do not need to do everything at once. Choose one idea that feels manageable today.

☾

Take a pause

Step away for a few minutes, breathe slowly and notice what you are feeling without judging it.

♡

Talk to someone

Share what is going on with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, counsellor or another safe adult.

☼

Protect your energy

Make time for rest, hobbies, movement and people who help you feel safe and understood.

When things feel heavy

You do not have to carry it alone.

It is okay to need help. If your feelings are becoming hard to manage, talk to someone you trust or reach out to a support service.

In New Zealand, you can call or text 1737 any time to talk with a trained counsellor. If you are in immediate danger, call 111.

Useful reminders

Small habits, real care.

🌿 Check in with yourself

Ask yourself: “What am I feeling right now, and what might help?”

💧 Give yourself a reset

Drink water, eat something nourishing if you need it, stretch or get some fresh air.

📵 Make room to rest

Take a break from scrolling or from a situation that is making you feel overwhelmed.

🤝 Ask for support

You do not have to wait until things are unbearable before talking to someone.
