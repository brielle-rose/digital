WELLBEING

Your feelings matter too.

Putting everyone else first can affect how you feel.

Stress

Constantly worrying about what other people think can become stressful and overwhelming.

Feeling Drained

Always saying yes and helping others can leave you feeling tired when you haven't made time for yourself.

Self-Worth

Depending on other people's approval can make it harder to recognise your own value.

Boundaries

Learning to communicate your needs can help you create healthier boundaries.

Need someone to talk to?

You don't have to deal with everything by yourself. Talking to someone you trust can be a good first step.
