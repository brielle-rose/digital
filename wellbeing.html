Your wellbeing matters

Look after the person who looks after everyone.

Your mental and emotional wellbeing deserves attention, too. Discover simple ways to check in with yourself and find support when things feel difficult.

Check in with yourself

How have you really been feeling?

You don't have to feel okay all the time. Taking a moment to recognise your feelings can help you understand what you need.

☁

Feeling overwhelmed?

Pause, take a few slow breaths, and focus on one thing at a time.

♡

Feeling guilty?

Remind yourself that having limits does not make you a bad friend or family member.

☀

Feeling exhausted?

Rest is important. You do not have to earn a break by doing everything first.

✿

Feeling unheard?

Consider talking to someone you trust who can listen without judging you.

Make time for yourself

Small acts of self-care

Self-care does not have to be expensive or complicated.

01

Take a short walk or spend a little time outside.

02

Listen to music, draw, write, pray, or do something you enjoy.

03

Take a break from social media when it starts to feel overwhelming.

04

Talk to someone you trust instead of keeping everything inside.

05

Give yourself permission to rest without feeling you must be productive.

Support is available

You don't have to face everything alone.

If your feelings are becoming difficult to manage, reach out to a trusted adult, school counsellor, health professional, or support service.
