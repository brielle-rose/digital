You are not alone

Experiences

Real feelings. Everyday situations. A reminder that you are not the only one learning how to put yourself first sometimes.

Does this feel familiar?

Little moments that can add up.

These are example situations, not real testimonials. You might recognise one, several or none of them.

“I don't want them to be upset with me.”

You agree to something you do not want to do because saying no feels like risking the friendship. You can care about someone and still be honest about what works for you.

“I'll just do it myself.”

You take on extra tasks because you worry people will think you are difficult. Sharing responsibility is fair, and asking for help is not a weakness.

“I'm fine — don't worry about me.”

You hide how you feel so nobody has to worry. You deserve support too, even when you are usually the person everyone comes to.

Try a reflection

Ask yourself: what do I need?

When you notice yourself automatically agreeing, pause for a moment. You can ask yourself:

  • Do I genuinely want to say yes?
  • Am I worried about their reaction?
  • What would I say if I knew my feelings mattered too?

There is no need to judge your answer. Noticing is already a useful step.
