You are not alone

Behind every smile, there may be a story.

From friendships to school and family expectations, people-pleasing can show up in everyday life. These examples may help you recognise feelings you have experienced yourself.

Everyday situations

Does any of this feel familiar?

These fictional scenarios are examples for reflection, not actual personal testimonies.

01 · FRIENDSHIPS ♡

The friend who always says yes

“I didn't want to go, but I said yes because I was scared my friends would think I didn't care.”

Sometimes the fear of being left out makes it difficult to be honest about what you want.

A gentle reminder

Real friendship should leave room for your needs and your boundaries.

02 · SCHOOL ✦

The student who does everything

“I already had too much work, but I took on another task because I didn't want to let anyone down.”

Wanting to be dependable can sometimes lead to stress, exhaustion, and putting your own needs last.

A gentle reminder

You can be responsible without having to do everything.

03 · FAMILY ☁

The one who hides their feelings

“I said I was fine because I didn't want to worry anyone or make things harder for my family.”

Hiding difficult feelings may feel easier in the moment, but you deserve support as well.

A gentle reminder

You can love your family and still need someone to listen to you.

You don't have to hide how you feel just to make everyone else comfortable.
