EXPERIENCES

You are not the only one.

People-pleasing can show up in everyday situations.

With Friends

You might agree with your friends even when you have a different opinion because you don't want to feel left out.

At School

You may take on extra work or responsibilities because you're worried about letting someone down.

With Family

You might hide how you really feel because you don't want to create conflict or disappoint someone you love.

Online

Social media can create pressure to look happy, fit in and be liked by others.

"You don't have to change who you are to be accepted."
