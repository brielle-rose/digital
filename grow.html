Progress over perfection

Grow at your own pace.

Building confidence and boundaries takes practice. You can start small, learn as you go and still be a kind person.

Your next small step

Try this, one step at a time.

You do not need to change everything overnight. Start with one small thing.

Notice the feeling

Before saying yes, notice if you feel excited, pressured, worried or unsure. You are allowed to take a moment.

Pause before answering

Try saying, “Let me think about it and get back to you.” You do not have to give an answer straight away.

Practise a kind boundary

Use simple words: “I can't this time,” or “I can help for 20 minutes, but not longer.”

Words you can borrow

Boundaries can be kind and clear.

If saying no feels difficult, practise a sentence that feels natural for you. You do not need a long explanation for every decision.

“Thanks for asking, but I can't this time.”

“I need some time to think about it.”

“I care about you, but I need a break right now.”

You can be a good person without being available to everyone, all the time.

Growth is about making room for yourself, too.

Keep showing up for yourself.

Come back to these reminders whenever you need them.
