GROW

Choose yourself too.

Small steps can help you become more confident in who you are.

01

Learn to say no

Saying no does not make you selfish. You are allowed to protect your time and energy.

02

Set boundaries

Be honest about what you are comfortable with and communicate your limits.

03

Listen to yourself

Pay attention to how you feel instead of automatically putting everyone else's feelings first.

04

Remember your worth

You do not need everyone's approval to know that you are valuable.

You are allowed to be yourself.

Your kindness matters — but so do you.
